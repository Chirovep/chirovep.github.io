01

Independent developer · 2026 · Vitality Hospital

Biometric attendance that pays for itself.

One tap at the door. A payroll-ready record by month end.

Proposal ↗

A hospital attendance system built around a browser kiosk and a manager dashboard. Staff tap once, look at the camera, and the server identifies them against every enrolled face — no typed ID, no shared PIN, no paper register. The system then decides clock-in or clock-out, applies the right shift window and produces the record HR needs.

  • Server-side 1:N face matching using 128-value descriptors; the kiosk never sends an employee ID, so a compromised tablet cannot name a person.
  • Signed, single-use liveness challenge — a random head turn or smile defeats printed photos and still phone screens; the method is stored on every attendance row.
  • Overnight-aware attendance engine — shift categories and per-employee overrides, time-zone-correct calculations in Africa/Harare, grace periods, early departure and an auto-logout cron for forgotten clock-outs.
  • Payroll and reporting — daily, weekly, monthly and custom ranges; per-employee summaries; CSV exports and a payroll API; every export recorded in history.

Technical note

Identity & liveness

Face enrolment stores multiple descriptors per employee. The punch endpoint performs 1:N matching, applies a configurable threshold, checks a signed liveness challenge and records both the match and the liveness method. Denied punches are audited.

Attendance maths

Shift windows are anchored to the clock-in day so a 22:00–06:00 shift closes the next morning, not at midnight. Working days, unpaid rest days and time-zone boundaries are handled explicitly.

Security

JWT sessions with 8-hour expiry, bcrypt work factor 12, HR_ADMIN and SYS_ADMIN roles, account lockout after five failures, rate-limited auth endpoints, Helmet headers, TLS 1.2+ and a provisioned device secret per kiosk.

Delivery

Docker Compose stack (MySQL, API, nginx), health checks, migrations and seeding scripts, 252 backend and 82 frontend tests, Playwright browser tests, secret scanning and a deployment pipeline gated on tests.

02

Full-stack developer · 2026 · Dzimba Bindura

A marketplace where rooms, bookings and money agree.

Housing, short stays and event tickets for Bindura — with payments and escrow built in.

Project notes ↓

A multi-role property and events platform: seekers find rentals, short stays and concert tickets; owners manage listings, rooms and bookings; admins verify identities, moderate content, release escrow payouts and check tickets at the gate. The hard part is not the listing page — it is keeping the money, the booking state and the audit trail consistent.

  • Payments that survive the real world — Paynow integration across EcoCash, OneMoney, card and bank, with webhook verification, a payment event log, retry-safe status polling and a payment state machine.
  • First month's rent in escrow — held on the platform, released only after move-in confirmation, with a payout workflow and a permanent record of who was paid, when and by whom.
  • Identity and trust — ID and selfie verification with face-match scoring, admin review queues, moderation notices, bans, listing takedowns and a tamper-evident audit log.
  • Beyond the listing — in-app messaging, Agora voice calls, QR-coded PDF tickets with gate check-in, OTP second-factor sign-in, password resets and account data export.

Technical note

Data model

20+ Prisma models covering users, listings, rooms, bookings, conversations, messages, voice calls, concerts, tickets, payments, payment events, verification records, moderation and audit logs, with indexes chosen for the queries the UI actually runs.

Money handling

Payments are written before the redirect, confirmed by webhook or polling, and every state change appends a PaymentEvent. Ticket issuance and booking confirmation happen only after a confirmed payment, so a refresh or a duplicate callback cannot double-sell.

Roles & access

SEEKER, OWNER and ADMIN scopes enforced on the server, not just hidden in the UI. Verification gates publishing; deactivation blocks access immediately; sessions are revoked by bumping a session epoch.

Operations

Prisma migrations and idempotent seed data, a health endpoint that reports database and integration status, backup scripts, Docker images and an nginx reverse proxy in front of the app.
